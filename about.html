Our story

A little café built around good food, great coffee, and good company.

How it started

Café Verde began with a simple idea: make a place where good coffee and honest food come first, and everything else — the lighting, the music, the worn wooden tables — supports that.

What started as a small counter has grown into a neighborhood gathering spot, but the goal hasn't changed. We still roast in small batches, prep our food fresh each morning, and greet regulars by name.

Every detail here, from the menu to the playlist, is something we've chosen with care — and we're always tweaking it as we learn what our neighbors love most.
